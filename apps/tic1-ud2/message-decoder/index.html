Let’s practise · Message decoder

Decode your secret message

Your initial changes the code, so different students may receive different hexadecimal messages.

How the code works

Use a circular alphabet with 27 symbols: the letters A–Z followed by a space. The messages are in English, so the alphabet does not include Ñ or accented letters. Your initial is assigned 0x00, the next symbol is 0x01, and the sequence continues around to A after the space.

For each character, the encoded value is c = (p - k + 27) % 27. Here, p is the character position and k is the position of your initial, with A = 0.
